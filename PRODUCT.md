# Product

**User.** People who play incremental games in the browser: the *Universal Paperclips*, *Cookie Clicker* and *A Dark Room* crowd, on a phone or a laptop. Also politics- and AI-curious players who want one hopeful picture of how public AI and robots could work. Today they get idle games with no idea in them, or policy essays nobody finishes.

**Painful job.** "Give me a 20-minute game with a twist that I can beat, brag about, and replay faster."

**Promise.** One citizen, one mandate, an automated future that belongs to everyone. You can finish it in a sitting, share your time, and beat a friend's.

**Wedge.**
- The finish-time card ("I reached Heaven in 21:30") and a challenge link that shows the next player the time to beat.
- A real speedrun guide whose route times are computed from the game's own rules.
- Searches for idle, clicker and incremental games; GitHub's incremental-game topics.

**Why now.** The argument about AI, automation and work is everywhere, and almost no game imagines the accountable, publicly owned version.

**Activation metric.** `run_complete`: a player reaches *Heaven is a metric*. The swing factor is shares per completion (`share_*` / `run_complete`). Both are instrumented and count once the GoatCounter key is set.

**First revenue event.** The first pay-what-you-want payment on an itch.io page, through the footer support link (OPERATIONS.md, keys 2 and 4). Pages itself stays non-commercial.

**What v1 deliberately won't do.**
- No accounts.
- No global leaderboard: it needs a server, and challenge links get the bragging loop without one.
- No ads, no cookies, no data collection.
- No donation, sign-up or voter-contact feature, and no tie to any real party, candidate, campaign or movement.
- No paid tier.
